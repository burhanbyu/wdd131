About Our Organization

Canada Refugee Pathway Support was founded by a former refugee and BYU-Pathway student who understands the challenges faced by displaced people seeking safety and opportunity.

Having experienced hardship while living in Kenya, the founder created this organization to help refugees access accurate, trustworthy, and easy-to-understand information about refugee pathways and settlement support in Canada.
