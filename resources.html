Settlement Resources for Newcomers

Moving to a new country can be challenging. These resources can help refugees and newcomers settle successfully in Canada.

Housing Support

Finding safe and affordable housing is one of the first steps after arriving in Canada.

Settlement agencies can help newcomers locate temporary accommodation and rental housing.

Healthcare Services

Refugees may be eligible for healthcare coverage through provincial and federal programs.

Access to doctors, hospitals, mental health services, and emergency care is available.

Employment Support

  • Resume writing assistance
  • Interview preparation
  • Job search support
  • Skills training programs
  • Career counseling

Education Opportunities

  • Public schools
  • Colleges and universities
  • Language classes
  • Adult education programs

Community Support Services

  • Settlement counseling
  • Language training
  • Family support services
  • Community events

Financial Literacy

Understanding banking, budgeting, and taxes is essential for success in Canada.
