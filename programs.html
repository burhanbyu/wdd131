Refugee Programs in Canada

Learn about the major refugee pathways available for individuals seeking protection and a new beginning in Canada.

Government-Assisted Refugees (GAR)

Government-Assisted Refugees are referred by international organizations like UNHCR.

The government provides financial and settlement support during the first year.

Private Sponsorship of Refugees (PSR)

Canadian citizens and organizations can sponsor refugees.

Sponsors help with financial and social settlement support.

Blended Visa Office-Referred (BVOR)

A shared program between government and private sponsors.

Required Documents

  • Identity documents
  • Birth certificates
  • Travel documents
  • Family records

Processing Times

  • Program type
  • Country of origin
  • Security screening
  • Medical exams
